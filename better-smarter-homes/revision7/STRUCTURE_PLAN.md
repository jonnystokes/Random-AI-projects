# Revision 7 Structure Plan

Last updated: 2026-07-12

Revision 7 reorganizes the 21 Revision 6 chapters into 26 chapters across three Parts and adds five new chapters (the fifth, Owner-Made Blocks and Alternative Concretes, added 2026-07-12 for the owner's make-your-own-materials focus). This file is the order, the file-rename map, and the reorganization decisions. It is the reference for R7-1 (reorganize) and R7-4 (write the new chapters). Front matter and appendices are deferred until chapters 1-25 are final.

## Reorganization Decisions (locked with the owner, 2026-07-12)

1. **Wall system — keep default, add double-wythe.** Single-wythe CMU + exterior insulation stays the book's default because it has the prescriptive IRC/ORSC path. The double-wythe cavity wall the owner most wants to build (inner CMU that the owner makes and stacks, cavity insulation, outer brick that the owner stacks, tied together over foundation concrete) is added as a **complete, dedicated alternative path** — not a re-centering. No prescriptive path exists for loadbearing double-wythe, so that path is presented as engineered.
2. **Four new chapters** (build-order positions below): The Wall System (foundation to roofline), Masonry Craft (laying and stacking block and brick), Solar / Battery / Energy Independence, and Personal Project Finance.
3. **Aggressive reorder:** three Part dividers; a one-page build-sequence-at-a-glance overview early (in the how-to-use chapter); Interiors moved after Smart Home so Interiors closes the house on move-in readiness; the five new chapters inserted in build-order; and the chapter-4 / chapter-18 inspection overlap consolidated (detail into the inspection chapter, an overview and pointer left in the legal chapter). This intentionally reverses the Rev 6 "chapter-4 inspection table stays" hold, which was an explicitly Rev-7-deferred item.

## Content Moves And Rewrites (R7-1 and R7-2)

- **The double-wythe consolidation.** The double-wythe pieces are currently scattered: wall ties for cavity walls (structural ch6, section on cavity-wall ties), cavity insulation and brick-veneer service life and anchorage/weeps (envelope chapter), and owner-manufactured CMU (materials chapter). R7-2 pulls the *assembly design* into new ch8 (The Wall System) and the *hand-craft of laying* into new ch10 (Masonry Craft), each cross-referencing the single-wythe default, the CMU-manufacture section, and the structural/envelope chapters. Nothing is deleted; the single-wythe default stays in its home chapters.
- **The inspection consolidation.** Old ch4 ("Legal, Permits, Owner-Builder Rules, and Inspections") holds a permit/owner-builder core plus an inspection table and inspection detail that overlaps the dedicated inspection chapter (new ch22). R7-1/R7-2 keeps the legal/permit/owner-builder core and a brief inspection *overview + pointer* in ch4, and moves the detailed inspection-sequence material into ch22. Audit every "see the … chapter" and any section-number reference touched by the split.
- **The Interiors/Smart-Home reorder.** Old ch14 (Interiors) and ch15 (Smart Home) swap relative order so Interiors (now ch19) closes Part II on move-in readiness. Cross-references between them (smart locks ↔ doors; low-voltage rough-in) are topic-name, so they survive; confirm in the audit.
- **The early overview.** A one-page "build-at-a-glance" sequence (land-use → feasibility → permits → foundation → walls → dry-in → rough-in → finish → occupancy) lands in ch1, giving the start-to-finish reader the roadmap the detailed Build Sequence chapter (ch21) provides in full.
