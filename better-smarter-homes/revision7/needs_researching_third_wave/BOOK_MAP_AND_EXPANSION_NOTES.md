Overlaps to respect (avoid duplication when the new chapters are written):

The proposed Roof System chapter must consolidate roof-as-a-system (weather surface, roof-wall junctions, drainage, overhangs, green-roof buildup) and reference §6.10 rather than repeat it. Reassess whether Roof is a full chapter after reading the envelope chapter.

## Land, Site, and Feasibility [MAPPED]

Scope: the pre-purchase feasibility filter (severity bands; five dealbreakers: no zoning dwelling path, failed septic, no legal access, no usable water, prohibitive recorded restriction); climate and design loads (parcel-specific values with Oregon early-planning ranges); zoning and allowed use (rural zone families RR/MUA/F1-F2/EFU/urban); property rights, title, and recorded restrictions (mineral/timber/water rights, easements, CC&Rs, conservation easements); utilities and access with extension cost ranges; septic and water feasibility (evaluation, well path, cost anchors, O&M contract, landowner well permit ORS 537.747); topography/drainage/buildability; distance/logistics; a purchase-decision checklist.

Notable / feeds new chapters:
- §3.4 and §3.6 already state Oregon prior-appropriation water law and the exempt domestic well; Water Independence extends the rights tangle and adds rainwater/storage, does not repeat the parcel feasibility.
- §3.2 wildfire: SB 83 (2025) repealed the statewide wildfire hazard map; ORSC R327 now applies only where a jurisdiction adopts it (confirm in writing). Key input for Resilience.
- §3.7 solar orientation, cold-air pooling, slope aspect feed Passive and Bioclimatic Design.
- §3.3 outbuildings, testing space, staging, block-press allowance feed Developing the Site.
Expansion: land agent (N) expands finding/evaluating/acquiring parcels and zoning paths; do not duplicate the feasibility filter, extend it.
