## Section A: The Wall System (ch8) and Masonry Craft (ch9) — the double-wythe path

1. **Veneer versus structural wythe.** For the owner's inner-CMU + cavity insulation + outer-brick wall: does TMS 402 treat the outer brick as an anchored masonry veneer (non-loadbearing, Chapter 12) or as a structural wythe of a composite/noncomposite multi-wythe wall (Chapter 5)? State what changes structurally, in code path, and in engineering requirement between the two readings. Lands in: ch8.
2. **Tie systems and schedules.** Wall-tie and veneer-tie types (adjustable two-piece, wire, joint-reinforcement ladder/truss), spacing limits (TMS 402; IRC R703.8 for veneer), corrosion protection by exposure (hot-dip galvanized vs. stainless), and the thermal-bridging penalty of steel ties crossing the cavity insulation. Lands in: ch8.
3. **Cavity, drainage, and insulation.** Minimum air space, the drainage plane, weep spacing and type, base flashing, and which cavity insulations suit a drained-and-vented cavity (rigid foam vs. mineral-wool board vs. injected). Lands in: ch8.
4. **Differential movement.** Brick moisture expansion vs. CMU drying shrinkage: expansion joints in brick, control joints in CMU, spacing (BIA Technical Notes, NCMA/CMHA TEK), and how the ties accommodate movement. Lands in: ch8.
5. **Seismic in Oregon SDC D0/D1.** Veneer and multi-wythe anchorage seismic requirements (TMS 402 seismic provisions), prescriptive limits, and where engineering becomes mandatory for the double-wythe. Lands in: ch8.
6. **Foundation for the double-wythe.** Footing width and the brick-shelf/ledge detail to carry both wythes, the slab-edge thermal break at the wall base, and how this differs from the single-wythe default footing. Lands in: ch8 (cross-ref ch7).
7. **Mortar (ASTM C270).** Mortar types M/S/N/O — which for loadbearing CMU, for brick veneer, and below grade; proportion vs. property spec; mixing, board life, and retempering rules. Lands in: ch9.
8. **Grout (ASTM C476).** Fine vs. coarse grout, slump, lift heights, consolidation and reconsolidation, low-lift vs. high-lift grouting, and cleanout requirements. Lands in: ch9.
9. **Laying technique and tolerances.** Coursing, bed and head joints, story poles, leads and lines, TMS 602 dimensional tolerances, plumb/level control, and joint tooling profiles (concave, V) and when each is used. Lands in: ch9.
10. **Weeps, flashing, and drainage at the craft level.** Through-wall flashing materials and lap detailing, weep spacing and type (open head joint, rope, vented), and mortar-dropping control (mortar net). Lands in: ch9.
11. **Cold- and hot-weather masonry.** TMS 602 construction and protection procedures by temperature band, admixtures, and heating/protection methods. Lands in: ch9.
12. **Reinforcement placement in masonry.** Vertical bar positioning and cover in cells, horizontal joint reinforcement vs. bond-beam bars, and lap splices in grout. Lands in: ch9 (cross-ref ch6).

## Section B: Solar, Battery, and Energy Independence (ch15)

13. **Oregon net-metering and interconnection.** The OPUC net-metering rules, utility interconnection process and agreement, system-size caps, and the timeline. Lands in: ch15.
14. **PV and storage code.** NEC Article 690 (PV), 705 (interconnection), 706 (energy storage), rapid shutdown (690.12), and how the OESC adopts/amends them. Lands in: ch15.
15. **Roof mounting on the concrete house.** Attachment and flashing on a concrete/masonry roof vs. a trussed roof, ballasted vs. attached on a flat concrete roof, and the reinforced south-roof zone provisioned in the documentation chapter. Lands in: ch15 (cross-ref ch8/ch10).
16. **Off-grid vs. grid-tied vs. hybrid.** Sizing method (load analysis, Oregon sun-hours), battery chemistry and sizing, generator-backup integration, and the independence-vs-cost trade-off. Lands in: ch15 (expect OWNER-DESIGN-CHOICE).
17. **Permitting and incentives.** The electrical/structural permit path for PV + storage, and the current incentive landscape (Energy Trust of Oregon; the federal 25D sunset already researched in Wave 2). Lands in: ch15.
