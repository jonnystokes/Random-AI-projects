# About This Book

*Written for the author and their family, with the builder and the specialist in mind.*

A complete reference for building a home that lasts many lifetimes — written to be read by anyone, and used by professionals.

*Stone and Wire* is a personal design and engineering reference — a record of every decision made, every material chosen, and every reason behind them. It is the kind of book that should exist for every home built with intention, but rarely does.

It is written for the **author and their family**, with the builder and the specialist in mind. Anyone can follow the plain-language sections. The technical sections give a professional enough to act on.

---

## How this book is organized

**01 — Plain language first**

What this section is about, in words anyone can follow — no background required.

**02 — Technical detail second**

Specific terminology, materials, assemblies, and methods — precise enough for a builder or specialist to act on.

**03 — Numbers and codes last**

Measurements, specifications, and building code references. Technical terms are defined in parentheses the first time they appear.
