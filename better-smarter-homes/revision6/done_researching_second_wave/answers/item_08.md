## The amendments document that IS published: the code review committee matrix

An amendments document does exist on BCD's server, unlinked from the pages above: "2025 Oregon Mechanical Specialty Code — Code review committee matrix," dated September 2024, 4 pages, at https://www.oregon.gov/bcd/codes-stand/Documents/25omsc-matrix.pdf (fetched and read in full 2026-07-09). Its header: "This matrix is a summary of the code review committee's recommendation to the Mechanical Board for the adoption of the 2025 OMSC," with outcome column per section (Retain ORA / Approve New ORA / Approve IMC / Rescind ORA / Disapprove).

The matrix outcomes became the adopted amendment set: the permanent rule filing "2025 Oregon Mechanical Specialty Code Adoption" (Permanent Administrative Order BCD 10-2025, filed 09/26/2025, effective 10/01/2025, amending OAR 918-440-0010) states the Mechanical Board "voted to approve the committee's recommendations and proceed to rulemaking" at its Oct. 22, 2024 meeting, and the Residential and Manufactured Structures Board approved the mechanical provisions for low-rise residential on Jan. 8, 2025 (https://www.oregon.gov/bcd/laws-rules/Documents/20251001-25omsc-adoption-pr.pdf).

## Matrix items touching dwelling ventilation

All from https://www.oregon.gov/bcd/codes-stand/Documents/25omsc-matrix.pdf (September 2024):

- Section 401.2 Ventilation required: two old Oregon amendments rescinded (the OMSC no longer points to the OSSC for natural ventilation; the OSSC now references the OMSC, and the prior deletion of the dwelling-units sentence is rescinded). New Oregon amendment: "Adopt ventilation in dwelling unit requirement, but remove reference to Energy Code and ASHRAE 90.1." Outcome: Approve New ORA.
- Section 402 Natural ventilation: IMC Sections 402.1-402.4 adopted (previous Oregon non-adoption note rescinded).
- Section 403 Mechanical ventilation, Section 403.3.2.1 "Outdoor air for dwellings units": IMC change M19-21 revised the dwelling-unit outdoor-air equation coefficient from 0.01 x Afloor to 0.03 x Afloor; "BCD recommends changing it to 0.02 Afloor." Outcome: "Approve IMC as modified by ORA," fiscal note "$0 - $400 for 0.02 / $0 - $800 for 0.03." The board-approved modification is the 0.02 coefficient, but the matrix's fiscal column keeps both figures, and the final printed coefficient can only be confirmed in the codebook itself (codes.iccsafe.org blocked). Flag for the design pass: verify 0.02 vs 0.03 in the printed 403.3.2.1 before sizing whole-house ventilation.
- Table 403.3.1.1 Minimum Ventilation Rates: existing Oregon footnote (g) retained/reworded; footnotes (n) and (m) added "for toilet rooms and bathrooms in private dwellings, single and multiple." Ventilation rates updated to ASHRAE Standard 62.1-2019 (M20-21, M21-21, M22-21).
- Table 403.3.2.3 Minimum Required Local Exhaust Rates for Group R-2, R-3 and R-4 occupancies: BCD adds the footnote requiring that "All rooms containing bathing or residential spa facilities with intermittent exhaust shall be provided with a mechanical ventilation system controlled by a dehumidistat, timer or similar means of automatic control."
- Section 501.3.1 Location of exhaust outlets: IMC M16-21/M28-21 adopted as modified; factory-built combination intake/exhaust termination fittings serving dwelling-unit ventilation systems no longer need special approval when installed per manufacturer instructions; committee added the option to use ASHRAE 62.1 Normative Appendix B.
- Section 501.6 Common ducts: IMC M29-21 Part I adopted; common exhaust ducts serving separate dwelling or sleeping units shall be connected where maintained at negative pressure (positive-pressure exhaust ducts cannot be joined).

## Matrix items touching dryer exhaust

Section 504 (clothes dryer exhaust) carries only retained existing Oregon amendments, no new ones (https://www.oregon.gov/bcd/codes-stand/Documents/25omsc-matrix.pdf):

- Section 504.9.2 Duct installation: "Removes the last sentence regarding screws or similar fasteners in domestic duct installation." Outcome: Retain ORA. (This is the long-standing Oregon amendment keeping screw points out of dryer duct interiors.)
- Section 504.9.3 Transition ducts: "Removes UL 2158A reference" (UL 2158A is the clothes-dryer transition-duct standard). Outcome: Retain ORA.
