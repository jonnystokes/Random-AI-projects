**Answered (2026-07-09):**

**Bottom line for citations: use ICC A117.1-2017 section numbers.** The accessibility standard Oregon's current codes reference is the 2017 edition; under the newest commercial code it is formally "ICC A117.1-2017 with Supplement 1", and Supplement 1 (approved 2024) only adds adult-changing-station provisions, so ordinary accessibility citations use unchanged 2017 section numbers. There is no 2024 edition of A117.1; the next full edition is designated the 2026 edition and is still unpublished as of today.

**Correction to this item's premise: the 2023 ORSC does not reference ICC A117.1 at all.** The expected Chapter 44 entry does not exist. Checked https://up.codes/viewer/oregon/irc-2021/chapter/44/referenced-standards on 2026-07-09: zero matches for "A117" anywhere in the served page or its data payload (caveat: UpCodes lazy-loads long chapters, so this alone is not conclusive, but it is corroborated by the code text itself). No ORSC section cites the standard. Oregon rewrote the ORSC accessibility section R320 (https://up.codes/viewer/oregon/irc-2021/chapter/3/building-planning, fetched 2026-07-09): R320.1 Scope reads, verbatim, "Dwelling units required to be accessible by ORS 447.231 shall comply with Chapter 11 of the Building Code as applicable."; R320.2 (live/work units) points to Building Code Sections 508.5.9, 508.5.11 and 1108.6.2.1; and the new R320.3 (attached stacked two-family dwellings, added by amendment permanent April 1, 2025) routes to the Fair Housing Act and "Chapter 11 of the Building Code" (/home/droid/theBook/revision6_code_library/23orsc-stacked-amend.pdf, page 3). ORS 447.231 ("Rules to eliminate architectural barriers") names no standard itself (/home/droid/theBook/revision6_code_library/ors/ors447.html). So A117.1 reaches ORSC-scope projects only indirectly, through OSSC Chapter 11, and only where ORS 447.231 or the Fair Housing Act applies; a detached one- or two-family owner-built dwelling carries no A117.1 code obligation, and the manuscript can present the standard as a voluntary design reference for such homes.

**Which edition Oregon's Building Code (OSSC) references.** The 2022 OSSC (based on the 2021 IBC, the Building Code until the 2025 edition took over) lists "ICC A117.1—17", i.e. the 2017 edition, in its Chapter 35 referenced standards (https://up.codes/viewer/oregon/ibc-2021/chapter/35/referenced-standards, fetched 2026-07-09). The 2025 OSSC (based on the 2024 IBC; effective Oct. 1, 2025 with a 6-month phase-in, mandatory April 1, 2026, per https://www.oregon.gov/bcd/codes-stand/Pages/adopted-codes.aspx) lists in Chapter 35, verbatim: "ICC A117.1—17 with Supplement 1, Standard for Accessible and Usable Buildings and Facilities", with referencing sections including 202, 1009.8.2, 1009.9, 1009.11, 1010.2.12.1, 1012.1, 1012.6.5, 1012.10, 1013.4, 1023.9, 1102.1, 1102.1.1, 1102.1.2 through 1102.1.2.10, 1108.2, 1110.1 and 1110.2 (list continues; https://up.codes/viewer/oregon/ibc-2024/chapter/35/referenced-standards, fetched 2026-07-09). Consistent with this history, UpCodes hosts three "Oregon Accessibility Code" texts, the 2009, 2017, and 2017-with-Supplement-1 editions (https://up.codes/codes/oregon); the 2009 edition belongs to the pre-2022 OSSC generation (2019 OSSC, 2018 IBC base) and should not be cited for current work.

**Status of the post-2017 editions.** There is no 2024 edition of ICC A117.1. The two post-2017 developments, per the ICC standard development page (https://www.iccsafe.org/icc-asc-a117-1/, fetched 2026-07-09): (1) Supplement 1 to the 2017 edition, approved by the A117.1 committee and ANSI in 2024, limited to technical provisions for adult changing stations; ICC packages it with the 2024 IBC as "A117.1-2017 with Supplement 1" (see also https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IBCACCPB2024P1/supplement-1-to-a117-1-2017-edition). (2) The next full edition, designated the 2026 edition: its final draft closed public comment Nov. 5, 2025; the committee completed final-ballot comment review on March 12, 2026 and submitted the 2026 edition for ANSI approval; ICC projects availability in late summer or early fall 2026. As of 2026-07-09 the 2026 edition is unpublished and is adopted nowhere in Oregon.

**Dependencies and recheck trigger.** Cite "ICC A117.1-2017", adding "with Supplement 1 (2024)" only where a passage concerns adult changing stations under the 2025 OSSC. Edition risk is on the commercial side only: Oregon will not reference the 2026 edition until a future OSSC adoption cycle after that edition publishes, and the pending 2026 ORSC (anticipated Oct. 1, 2026, 2024 IRC base, https://www.oregon.gov/bcd/codes-stand/Pages/orsc-adoption.aspx) changes nothing here because the residential code's accessibility path still routes through ORS 447.231 to the Building Code rather than to A117.1 directly.
