# Item 16: EV-ready and solar-ready provisions for one- and two-family dwellings (2023 ORSC)

**Bottom line: No EV-ready requirement and no solar-ready requirement applies to detached one- and two-family dwellings under the 2023 ORSC. Oregon's EV-ready mandate attaches only to private commercial, five-or-more-unit multifamily, and mixed-use (commercial plus five or more units) buildings, through the OSSC, and the statute expressly forbids applying it to townhouses. The IRC solar-ready appendix (Appendix AT) is affirmatively not adopted in the ORSC, and local adoption of it is preempted statewide.**

## 1. EV-ready: the statute (HB 2180, 2021)

- HB 2180 (2021 Regular Session), enrolled text: https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2021R1/Downloads/MeasureDocument/HB2180/Enrolled. Codified as ORS 455.417 "Provision of electric service capacity for charging electric vehicles in newly constructed buildings; requirements; exemptions; rules" [2021 c.152 §1]. Full text in the local library: `/home/droid/theBook/revision6_code_library/ors/ors455.html` (also at https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors455.html).
- ORS 455.417(2): the DCBS director shall amend the state building code to require covered new buildings to include "provisions for electrical service capacity" for EV charging at "no less than 20 percent of the vehicle parking spaces in the garage or parking area for the building"; fractional results round up.
- ORS 455.417(1)(c) defines "provisions for electrical service capacity" as service capacity (or designated space or a designated remote location) plus a conduit system to the parking spaces able to support Level 2 charging wiring, with conduit labeled at both ends when installed for future charging stations.
- ORS 455.417(3)(a) limits the code requirement to exactly three building types: "(A) Commercial buildings under private ownership; (B) Multifamily residential buildings with five or more residential dwelling units; and (C) Mixed-use buildings consisting of privately owned commercial space and five or more residential dwelling units."
- ORS 455.417(3)(b): "The director may not make code requirements under subsection (2) of this section applicable to townhouses."
- ORS 455.417(4): a municipality may, through its land-use process, require more than 20 percent for the covered building types (it cannot extend the mandate to dwellings).
- One- and two-family dwellings are therefore outside the statutory authorization entirely, and townhouses are excluded by name.

## 2. EV-ready: the implementing rule (OSSC, not ORSC)

- OAR 918-460-0200 "Electric Vehicle Charging Station Infrastructure Requirements" (Permanent Administrative Order BCD 6-2022; adopt filed 06/30/2022; effective 07/01/2022) amends the Oregon Structural Specialty Code to require the 20 percent EV-ready parking provision for the same three building categories, excluding townhouses. Official current text: https://secure.sos.state.or.us/oard/viewSingleRule.action?ruleVrsnRsn=291676; mirror: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/oregon/Or-Admin-Code-SS-918-460-0200. Statutory authority listed: ORS 455.030 and ORS 455.417.
- Division 918-460 is the OSSC (commercial) program division. BCD implemented HB 2180 by rule against the OSSC; no parallel rule amends the ORSC (division 918-480), consistent with ORS 455.417(3).
- A live BCD "EV-ready" program page could not be located as of 2026-07-09: the BCD notice PDF previously indexed at oregon.gov/bcd/laws-rules/Documents/20220701-hb2180-evcharging-pr.pdf returns 404, and no ev-ready.aspx page exists under oregon.gov/bcd. The operative citations are the statute and OAR above.

## 3. ORSC 2023 Chapter 11: no EV or solar content

- The 2023 ORSC's operative energy chapter is Oregon-written. Its sections are N1101 Scope, N1102 Definitions, N1103 Alternative Systems, N1104 Exterior Envelope Requirements, N1105 Heating, Ventilating and Air-Conditioning Systems, N1106 Piping Insulation, N1107 Lighting and Power, N1108 Plumbing Fixture Efficiency, plus NA1109 Energy Rating Index (ERI) Compliance and NF1110 through NF1116 (window thermal performance and air leakage). None of these sections contains any electric-vehicle, vehicle-charging, solar, or solar-ready provision. Source (fetched 2026-07-09): https://up.codes/viewer/oregon/irc-2021/chapter/new_11/energy-efficiency (note: UpCodes hosts Oregon's replacement chapter at the `new_11` slug; the plain `chapter/11/energy-efficiency` entry is the unamended IRC [RE] shell).
- Compliance basis confirming this is the operative chapter: N1101.1 requires conditioned spaces to comply with Table N1101.1(1) plus one additional measure from Table N1101.1(2); the verbatim Table N1101.1(1) values are in the BCD technical bulletin in the local library: `/home/droid/theBook/revision6_code_library/res-techb-slabedge-insulation.pdf`. The ORSC errata touch N1101, N1104, and N1105 only: `/home/droid/theBook/revision6_code_library/23orsc-errata.pdf`.

## 4. Solar-ready appendix: present in the code book, affirmatively NOT adopted, and preempted

- The 2021 IRC model appendix exists in the ORSC book as Appendix AT [RE] "Solar-Ready Provisions—Detached One- and Two-Family Dwellings and Townhouses" (listed in the ORSC 2023 table of contents: https://up.codes/viewer/oregon/irc-2021).
- 2023 ORSC Section R101.2.3.2 "Matters preempted by the state building code" states: "The following matters are not adopted, are preempted by the state building code and may not be adopted by a local municipality," and its list includes item 14 "Appendix AT (Solar-ready Provisions—Detached One- and Two-family Dwellings and Townhouses)" and item 16 "Appendix AX (Zero Energy Residential Building Provisions)". Source: official BCD publication of ORSC Chapter 1, local library file `/home/droid/theBook/revision6_code_library/23orsc-ch1.pdf`.
- 2023 ORSC Section R101.2.4 "Appendices" lists the appendices adopted as part of the code: AE (Manufactured Housing), AF (Radon Control Methods), AH (Deck, Patio or Porch Covers), AK (Sound Transmission), AR (Light Straw-clay), AS (Strawbale), AU (Cob), AW (3D-printed). No solar-ready or energy appendix is adopted. Same source: `/home/droid/theBook/revision6_code_library/23orsc-ch1.pdf`.
- So solar-ready is not merely "optional" in Oregon: R101.2.3.2 blocks even local mandatory adoption. A homeowner may of course rough in conduit and reserve roof area voluntarily.
