**Answered (2026-07-09):**

**Bill and session law.** House Bill 3525, 2025 Regular Session, 83rd Oregon Legislative Assembly, "Relating to exempt wells that supply ground water for domestic purposes." Passed House June 20, 2025; passed Senate June 26, 2025; repassed House June 27, 2025 (enrolled HB 3525-C: https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2025R1/Downloads/MeasureDocument/HB3525; overview: https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2025R1/Measures/Overview/HB3525). Chaptered as Oregon Laws 2025, chapter 574: approved by the Governor July 24, 2025; filed with the Secretary of State July 28, 2025; effective date January 1, 2026; section 2 (the landlord testing duty) becomes operative January 1, 2027 under section 4 (official chapter print: https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/lawsstatutes/2025orLaw0574.pdf).

**Codification.** The duty is codified as ORS 90.321, "Testing of drinking water in ground water quality management area; report to tenants and Oregon Health Authority; rules; limits on data use" [2025 c.574 s.2; 2025 c.574 s.2a], in the residential landlord-tenant chapter (https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors090.html). The section 2a contingency was triggered because SB 1154 (2025) also became law as Oregon Laws 2025 chapter 605 (https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2025R1/Measures/Overview/SB1154), so the operative wording is "ground water quality management area" as defined in ORS 468B.150.

**Scope.** ORS 90.321(2) applies when a dwelling unit has an "exempt well" (a well used for purposes exempt under ORS 537.545(1)(b) or (d)) as a source of drinking water and the unit sits within a ground water quality management area under ORS 468B.150. ORS 90.321(11) excludes dwelling units on premises regulated as public water systems under ORS 448.119 to 448.285, 454.235 and 454.255 (https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors090.html).

**Test panel.** "Contaminants" includes arsenic, coliform bacteria, lead and nitrates: ORS 90.321(1)(a) (https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors090.html).

**Frequency.** Arsenic must be tested no later than 30 days after installing a new exempt well: ORS 90.321(3)(a). Otherwise each contaminant must be tested at least once each year: ORS 90.321(3)(b). A test with no contaminant above the current EPA maximum contaminant levels earns a four-year pause if it is the first test for the unit, the first test after a prior extension, or the second consecutive clean annual test after a failed test: ORS 90.321(4). After a failed test (any coliform, or any contaminant above EPA MCLs) the landlord must retest on a schedule set by OHA rule, notwithstanding (3) and (4): ORS 90.321(9)(c). Catch-up requirement: for each unit already subject on January 1, 2027, the landlord must sample and test for all contaminants before June 1, 2027 (Oregon Laws 2025 ch. 574 s.3, printed as a note after ORS 90.321) (https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors090.html).

**Sampling and laboratories.** Samples come from the unit's primary drinking and cooking faucet; collection may be delegated to the tenant only by a written, good-faith agreement for adequate consideration: ORS 90.321(5). The laboratory must be accredited under the ORS 438.615 environmental laboratory accreditation program and must report results electronically to OHA: ORS 90.321(6) (https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors090.html).

**Tenant disclosure.** Results go to the tenant within 30 days after the landlord receives them, as either the full laboratory report or an OHA pass/fail form: ORS 90.321(7). Before entering a rental agreement, the landlord must give written notice that the unit is on an exempt well in a ground water quality management area, the dates and results of the most recent test for each contaminant (or a statement it has not yet been tested), and the latest date of the next required test: ORS 90.321(8). After a failed test the landlord must also deliver an OHA health-effects handout as soon as practicable: ORS 90.321(9). OHA must adopt implementing rules for the form and handout: ORS 90.321(10); OHA may act before the operative date: ch. 574 s.4(2) (https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors090.html).

**Interaction with ORS 448.271.** The 2025 act does not amend ORS 448.271. ORS 448.271 remains the separate, statewide transaction-time duty: the seller of real estate that includes a domestic well, upon accepting an offer to purchase, must test the water for arsenic, nitrates and total coliform bacteria and submit the results to OHA and the buyer within 90 days of receiving them; noncompliance does not invalidate the conveyance; source line shows amendments through 2009 (2009 c.284 s.1; 2009 c.595 s.855) (https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_448.271). Differences worth stating in the manuscript: ORS 90.321 is a recurring duty during a tenancy, limited to exempt wells inside ground water quality management areas, and its panel adds lead; ORS 448.271 is a one-time at-sale duty for any domestic well statewide and does not include lead. Both statutes route results to OHA. An owner-builder who later rents out a house on an exempt well inside a ground water quality management area carries both duties, each at its own trigger.
