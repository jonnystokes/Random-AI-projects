## Mission

Revision 5 is the active rewrite of the Better Smarter Homes manuscript.

The goal is a large, practical, technically serious owner-builder manual for a concrete home in Oregon. It should be readable to curious non-builders, useful to the owner-builder doing the work, and strong enough to support conversations with trades, designers, engineers, inspectors, and suppliers.

## Recovery Protocol

After compaction or restart:

1. Use `AI_TODO.md` for the fresh-start reading order, workflow, active chapter, queue, and next action.
2. Use `agent.md` for the source rule.
3. Use `writing_style_guide.md` for all writing voice, layer, cross-reference, citation-format, and cleanup rules.
4. Use `SOURCE_CROSSWALK.md` for prior-revision source paths.
5. Use `STRUCTURE_PLAN.md` for chapter order and structure.
6. Use `CHANGELOG.md` only when historical context is needed.
