## Grading System

| Score | Verdict | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 5 | Yes | Priority image. The section explains spatial, layered, procedural, or unfamiliar construction information that a visual would clarify immediately. |
| 4 | Yes | Strong image candidate. A diagram, cutaway, workflow, or document spread would materially improve comprehension. |
| 3 | Yes | Useful support image. Worth illustrating if art budget allows or if paired with a nearby section. |
| 2 | Maybe | Low-priority visual. Could use a small icon, simple flow, or sidebar graphic, but text/table may be enough. |
| 1 | No | Mostly orientation, checklist, or record-keeping. Avoid standalone art unless needed for page rhythm. |
| 0 | No | Do not add art. Decorative image would distract or waste space. |

General art direction: prioritize clear cutaways, layered assemblies, process diagrams, field-tool photos, inspection hold-point visuals, annotated document spreads, and map-style decision graphics. Avoid generic house stock imagery.
