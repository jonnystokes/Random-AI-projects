# Stone & *Wire*

## Building a Home That Lasts Many Lifetimes

*A practical owner-builder guide to planning, designing, building, testing, and maintaining a durable home.*

---

## About This Book

This book is a design and build guide for a durable, serviceable, high-performance home. It explains the reasoning behind the house, the sequence for building it, and the records needed to maintain it over time.

The structure of the book now follows the work itself: first principles, land, rules, ground, structure, envelope, systems, interiors, automation, construction, testing, documentation, and business context.
