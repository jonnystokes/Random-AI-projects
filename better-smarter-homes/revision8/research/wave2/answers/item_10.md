**Answered (2026-07-09):**

**Governing rule set.** Oregon backflow tester certification and backflow assembly test timing live in OAR chapter 333, division 61 (Oregon Health Authority drinking water rules). The division applies to public water systems: OAR 333-061-0010 (Scope) states the rules apply to all public water systems providing piped water for human consumption (https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_333-061-0010). Statutory authority shown on the rules is ORS 448.131, plus ORS 448.279 for tester certification (https://secure.sos.state.or.us/oard/view.action?ruleNumber=333-061-0072).

**Test timing: at installation and annually.** OAR 333-061-0070 (Cross Connection Control Requirements), section (6): the water supplier must ensure that inspections of approved air gaps and inspections and tests of approved backflow prevention assemblies protecting the public water system are conducted: at the time of installation, repair or relocation; at least once every 12 months after installation; more frequently than every 12 months for assemblies that repeatedly fail or that protect health hazard cross connections, as determined by the water supplier; after a backflow incident; and after an approved air gap is re-plumbed (https://secure.sos.state.or.us/oard/view.action?ruleNumber=333-061-0070). The "at installation and annually" citation is therefore OAR 333-061-0070(6).

**Who must be certified.** OAR 333-061-0070(16): "Backflow prevention assemblies must be tested by Authority-certified Backflow Assembly Testers, journeyman plumbers or apprentice plumbers." The tester must produce three copies of each test report: one for the tester's permanent records, one for the water user or property owner, and one for the water supplier, submitted within 10 working days (https://secure.sos.state.or.us/oard/view.action?ruleNumber=333-061-0070). The certification rule itself is OAR 333-061-0072 (Backflow Assembly Tester Certification), section (1): "Only the following individuals may perform the field-testing on backflow prevention assemblies required by these rules: (a) Individuals certified by the Authority to test backflow prevention assemblies; and (b) Journeyman plumbers defined as those who hold a certificate of competency issued under ORS chapter 693 or apprentice plumbers." Section (2) requires journeyman or apprentice plumbers who test to complete an Authority-approved backflow assembly tester training course (https://secure.sos.state.or.us/oard/view.action?ruleNumber=333-061-0072). Certification mechanics under the same rule: Authority-approved training course, written exam score of at least 70 percent, physical-performance exam score of at least 90 percent, $195 fee, renewal every 2 years, with yearly test gauge accuracy verification or calibration reports required at renewal (https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_333-061-0072).

**Adjacent rules in the 0070-0074 block.** OAR 333-061-0071: Backflow Prevention Assembly Installation and Operation Standards (https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_333-061-0071). OAR 333-061-0073: Cross Connection Specialist Certification; only Authority-certified individuals may administer cross connection control programs (https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_333-061-0073). OAR 333-061-0074: Cross Connection Training Programs, Course, and Instructor Requirements (https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_333-061-0074).

**Public water: the obligation runs through the water supplier's program.** OAR 333-061-0070(10): in community water systems, water suppliers must implement a cross connection control program directly or by written agreement with another agency experienced in cross connection control. Section (6) frames the testing duty as one the water supplier "must ensure" is carried out. Where premises isolation is used, section (9)(b) places duties on the premises owner, who must "assume responsibility for installation, testing, maintenance, and repair of the installed approved backflow prevention assembly" (https://secure.sos.state.or.us/oard/view.action?ruleNumber=333-061-0070). Practical reading for the book: a home connected to a public water system gets the annual certified test because the serving water supplier's cross-connection program requires it of the customer, and the test report goes to the supplier within 10 working days.

**Private systems: no direct division 61 obligation.** Because division 61 applies to public water systems (OAR 333-061-0010, https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_333-061-0010), a home on its own private well is outside these rules; no supplier program attaches, and OHA imposes no certified-tester or annual-test duty on that owner under this division. Backflow protection on a private system remains an Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code installation requirement enforced through the plumbing permit; the exact OPSC section is not pinned here (code text paywalled) and any periodic-testing practice on private systems is a local matter.

**Rule currency.** OAR 333-061-0070 was most recently amended by PH 118-2024, filed 12/13/2024, effective 01/01/2025 (https://secure.sos.state.or.us/oard/view.action?ruleNumber=333-061-0070). OAR 333-061-0072 was most recently amended by PH 7-2023, filed 01/31/2023, effective 02/01/2023, with original adoption HD 1-1994 (https://secure.sos.state.or.us/oard/view.action?ruleNumber=333-061-0072).
