**(a) As-built plan at installation.** The rules use the term "as-built plan"; "record drawing" does not appear anywhere in division 71 or 73 (verified by search of both local files).
- OAR 340-071-0170(3)(a): to request the pre-cover inspection, the system installer must submit to the agent "a detailed and accurate as-built plan of the constructed system," plus (3)(b) a list of all materials used and (3)(c) a signed certification on a DEQ-approved form that the system was built as the permit, division 71, and division 73 require. (C:/Users/jon/Projects/theBook/revision6_code_library/oar/oar-340-071-full.html)
- OAR 340-071-0170(1)-(2): the pre-cover inspection is mandatory before backfill unless the agent waives it; waiver is allowed for single-family-dwelling systems installed by a sewage disposal service business licensed under ORS 454.695, with photographs submitted on request.
- OAR 340-071-0400(5)(d)(A): in low-precipitation areas east of the Cascades (parcels 10 acres or larger, 20 inches annual precipitation or less), the agent may waive the pre-cover inspection if the installer submits the same as-built plan, materials list, and certification. Geographic dependency: this waiver route exists only in those areas.
- OAR 340-071-0302(5)(a): for recirculating gravel filter (RGF) systems, the designer must give the owner and agent an operation and maintenance manual at installation that includes "as-built plans with the name and contact number of the installer." This is the only rule that puts an as-built directly in the owner's hands; for other system types the as-built goes into the agent's permit file.
- OAR 340-071-0345(13): for ATT systems the designer must give the owner a comprehensive owner's manual (system specifications, installation, O&M, troubleshooting) before or at installation; the rule does not use the term as-built.
- OAR 340-073 contains no as-built, record, or maintenance obligations; it is tank and component construction standards only (340-073-0025 through 340-073-0085, all amended effective 01/01/2026). (C:/Users/jon/Projects/theBook/revision6_code_library/oar/oar-340-073-full.html)

**(b) Certificate of Satisfactory Completion (CSC).** OAR 340-071-0175, amended effective 01/01/2026. (C:/Users/jon/Projects/theBook/revision6_code_library/oar/oar-340-071-full.html)
- 0175(1)-(2): agent issues the CSC after inspecting and finding the installation complies with divisions 71 and 73 and the permit conditions; otherwise a written notice or posted Correction Notice lists deficiencies and required corrections.
- 0175(3): if the agent does not inspect within 7 days after notification of completion, or waived inspection under 340-071-0170(2) or 340-071-0400(5)(d), the CSC "will be deemed to have been issued by operation of law" and a modified Certificate is issued to the owner.
- 0175(4): backfilling is allowed only after the agent declines to inspect, issues the CSC, or the CSC is deemed issued.
- 0175(5): the permittee must complete corrections within 30 days of a Correction Notice unless the agent allows more time.
- 0175(6): no one may connect to or use a system completed after January 1, 1974 without an issued or deemed-issued CSC. Companion owner duty: a system operated without a required CSC or Authorization Notice must be decommissioned, OAR 340-071-0185(1)(e).
- 0175(7): installer must backfill within 10 days after CSC issuance unless the agent requires otherwise.
- 0175(8): the CSC is valid for five years for connecting the system to the facility it was built for; after five years an Authorization Notice, alteration permit, or construction-installation permit may be required under OAR 340-071-0160, 0205, or 0210.
- OAR 340-071-0160(5): once the CSC is issued, its conditions "continue in force as long as the system is in use" (they follow the system, not the original owner).
- 0175(9): denial or revocation is appealable under ORS 183.310-183.550 and OAR 340 division 011.

**(c) Alternative treatment technology (ATT) and other alternative-system O&M obligations.** Core rule: OAR 340-071-0132 "Operation and Maintenance," a NEW rule adopted by DEQ 29-2025 effective 01/01/2026 (history line reads "adopt," the only new rule in the division 71 package). (C:/Users/jon/Projects/theBook/revision6_code_library/oar/oar-340-071-full.html)
- Who must have a service contract, 0132(1)(a): owners of all ATT systems, all RGFs, pressurized distribution and residential sand filter systems permitted after January 1, 2014, and commercial sand filters permitted on or after March 1, 2005 (systems not under WPCF permits) must (A) maintain a contract with a maintenance provider certified under OAR 340-071-0650, executed before the system is installed and kept until the system is decommissioned; (B) report system failures to the provider and the agent and take agent-approved corrective action (repair permit under OAR 340-071-0215 if needed); (C) ensure the system is inspected at least annually by a certified maintenance provider or by the agent under OAR 340-071-0260. ATT permit precondition: OAR 340-071-0345(1)(d) and (12).
- Contract content and service frequency, 0132(2): contracts must be "perpetual and continuous" until decommissioning; for newly permitted systems, inspect/adjust/service at least once every 6 months for the first two years (2)(a), then per the manufacturer's approved owner's manual and at least once every 12 months (2)(b). ATT contracts must include an effluent quality inspection clause (visual color/turbidity/scum, odor, and any needed performance assessment or effluent sampling), (2)(c). Provider must notify owner and agent in writing within 30 days of any problem not fixable at inspection, (2)(d).
- Provider duties, 0132(3): written condition reports to the owner (a); minor or major maintenance as defined in OAR 340-071-0100(92) (b); keep current plus previous 3 years of contracts, checklists, and performance data (c); written notice to owner and agent within 30 days of contract termination (d); emergency service within 48 hours (e); submit the annual report and pay the annual report evaluation fee under OAR 340-071-0140(3) for each system under contract (f); for ATTs, be certified by that ATT's manufacturer (h). Product dependency: certification and service details are per manufacturer and model; DEQ lists ATTs by brand and model under OAR 340-071-0345(2).
- Reporting frequency, 0132(4): once per year, by the date DEQ specifies, for each year the system is in operation; the maintenance provider (not the owner) submits the DEQ-form certification and pays the annual report evaluation fee, (4)(a)-(b). A system is "in operation" from CSC issuance or plumbing connection, whichever is later, until the agent is notified of decommissioning, (4)(a). Exceptions for new contracts late in the reporting period, (4)(c). Holding tanks are the exception where the owner still pays and reports annually, 0132(1)(c) and (4)(d).
- Enforcement, 0132(6) and OAR 340-071-0140(7): compliance recovery fee for late provider reports/fees, and if the owner fails to keep a contract (beyond a transition period of not more than 30 days) the agent may require a compliance inspection under OAR 340-071-0260 plus the compliance recovery fee. Owner pays all costs of inspections done by maintenance providers, OAR 340-071-0260(2)(c)(C).
- Fee amounts: the rule text gives categories only; dollar amounts live in Tables 9A-9F of OAR 340-071-0800, published as an attachment (not present in the SOS HTML). The fee-table PDF currently posted by DEQ (https://www.oregon.gov/deq/FilterDocs/onsiteRuleFeeTables.pdf) is the edition effective July 1, 2021: annual report evaluation for ATT/sand filter/pressurized distribution/RGF $62 hard copy or $52 online; holding tanks $31/$26; alternative system inspection $544; Existing System Evaluation Report $659 (Table 9A). Caveat: 340-071-0800 was amended again effective 11/01/2025 (DEQ 21-2025), 01/01/2026 (DEQ 29-2025), and 07/01/2026 (DEQ 2-2026), so current amounts likely differ; use the current Tables 9A-9F link on https://www.oregon.gov/deq/residential/pages/onsite-rules.aspx, and remember contract counties bill their own adopted fees plus the DEQ surcharge (OAR 340-071-0140(9)-(10)).

**(d) Owner obligations at sale or transfer.** Division 71 contains no general point-of-sale septic inspection or reporting requirement (searched "sale," "transfer," "ownership," "conveyance" in the full text). What does apply:
- Service-contract continuity: because the contract must run until decommissioning (OAR 340-071-0132(2)), the obligation lands on each successive owner. The rule allows a "contract transition period of not more than 30 days" before the agent may order a compliance inspection and fee, 0132(6)(b); DEQ's O&M fact sheet states it plainly: "If you sell or buy a property, or your contract is terminated, you or the new owner have 30 days to get under contract" (https://www.oregon.gov/deq/Residential/Documents/onsiteOpMaintReq2024fs.pdf).
- Unapproved-technology systems only: before transferring ownership, the owner must notify all transferees that the technology is not approved, and the transferee must agree in writing to hold the State of Oregon harmless, OAR 340-071-0135(4)(d).
- Voluntary at-sale tool: the Existing System Evaluation Report, OAR 340-071-0155, is the inspection product typically ordered in real-estate transactions; it is not mandated by the rules. Qualified evaluators are listed in 0155(1)(a); new for 2026, a NAWT-accredited inspector must also hold a sewage disposal service license (0155(1)(a)(E); DEQ general fact sheet).
- Related but not sale-triggered: an Authorization Notice is required before placing into service, reconnecting, connecting an accessory dwelling unit to, changing the use of, or increasing flow into an existing system, OAR 340-071-0205(1). A construction permit can be transferred to a new person (name change only) before it expires, OAR 340-071-0160(6)(b). CSC conditions run with the system in use, OAR 340-071-0160(5).

**(e) New in the 2026 rules on these topics** (per rule history lines and the two DEQ fact sheets):
- All O&M requirements consolidated into new rule OAR 340-071-0132 (adopted effective 01/01/2026); DEQ general fact sheet: "All O&M rules have been moved to the new Operation and Maintenance section: OAR 340-071-0132."
- Owners of contracted alternative systems no longer submit annual reports or pay the annual report evaluation fee; that duty moved solely to the maintenance provider, OAR 340-071-0132(3)(f) and (4)(a)-(b); DEQ O&M fact sheet: "No more annual reports or fees for owners." Holding-tank owners still report and pay, 0132(4)(d).
- Service contracts must now be perpetual and continuous with no automatic expiration; legacy contracts executed on or before December 31, 2025 "will expire on December 31, 2027" unless they meet the new 0132(2) requirements, OAR 340-071-0132(2). This is the hard transition deadline for existing ATT/sand filter/RGF/pressurized-distribution owners.
- 30-day contract transition window (including at sale or purchase) before compliance inspection and compliance recovery fee, OAR 340-071-0132(6)(b) and OAR 340-071-0140(7).
- Compliance recovery fee codified for late annual reports, missing service contracts, missing authorization notices, and unpermitted work, OAR 340-071-0140(7).
- Start-up inspection with a report on a manufacturer or DEQ-approved form may now be required from the certified maintenance provider for pressure distribution, sand filter, RGF, and ATT systems before the CSC is issued, OAR 340-071-0345(14)(c); DEQ O&M fact sheet.
- Provider recordkeeping set at current plus previous 3 years instead of indefinite, OAR 340-071-0132(3)(c); DEQ O&M fact sheet.
- Pre-cover inspection waiver for single-family systems installed by an ORS 454.695-licensed business, with photos on request, OAR 340-071-0170(2).
- Definition changes that touch owners: "Major Maintenance" (replacing distribution or drop boxes) needs no repair permit when done by a certified installer or certified maintenance provider with an SDS license, but a property owner doing that work personally still needs a Minor Repair permit, OAR 340-071-0100(92) and DEQ general fact sheet. New ADU definition with a 300 gpd minimum design flow (750 gpd for dwelling plus ADU), OAR 340-071-0100 and DEQ general fact sheet.
- OAR 340-073 changes are material standards only (siphons and steel septic tanks removed, pipe standards updated); none add owner record or maintenance obligations. (C:/Users/jon/Projects/theBook/revision6_code_library/oar/oar-340-073-full.html; DEQ general fact sheet)
