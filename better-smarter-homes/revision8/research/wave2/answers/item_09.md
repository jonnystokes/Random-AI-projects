## Shower and tub-shower valves: OPSC 408.3, confirmed

- Section number confirmed by an official source: BCD's 2021 OPSC Summary of Changes (the base edition the 2023 OPSC carries forward) places 408.3 in the hot-water temperature-limitation family and describes the one model-code change to it: a water heater conforming to ASSE 1084 became an alternative limiting means "versus the point of use 1070," and "This new allowance also is effective for sections 408.3, 409.4, and 410.3" (item 4, Section 407.3 entry): https://www.oregon.gov/bcd/codes-stand/Documents/21opsc-change-summary.pdf
- Requirement content (UPC model text, carried unamended in Oregon): showers and tub-shower combinations must have individual control valves of the pressure-balance, thermostatic, or combination pressure-balance/thermostatic mixing type that provide scald and thermal shock protection, complying with ASSE 1016/ASME A112.1016/CSA B125.16 or ASME A112.18.1/CSA B125.1, installed at the point of use, with the handle position stop or temperature-limiting control adjusted per the manufacturer's instructions to a maximum mixed-water setting of 120 degrees F (49 degrees C); the water heater thermostat is not an acceptable control for meeting the provision. UPC-family section text: https://up.codes/s/individual-shower-and-tub-shower-combination-control-valves
- Oregon plan-review practice quotes the same number and substance: Northwest Code Professionals (Oregon third-party inspection agency) residential requirements list, item 83: "OPSC 408.3 All shower heads/control valves shall be equipped with a pressure balance or thermostatic mixing control valve set or adjusted per the manufacturer's instructions for a maximum mixed water setting of 120 degrees": https://nwcodepros.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/2018-Residential-Construction-Requirements.pdf (2017-OPSC era document; the section number is unchanged through the 2021 and 2023 cycles per the BCD summaries above).

## Bathtub and whirlpool fill limit: OPSC 409.4, not a 424-series section

- The "expected 424 series" in the question is IPC numbering (IPC 424.3 individual shower valves, IPC-family 424.5 bathtub and whirlpool bathtub valves). The OPSC is UPC-based, so the tub limit lives at Section 409.4, Limitation of Hot Water in Bathtubs and Whirlpool Bathtubs. The cross-family contrast is visible on the section-share page, which shows 409.4 in UPC-based codes alongside 424.5 "Bathtub and Whirlpool Bathtub Valves" in IPC-based codes: https://up.codes/s/limitation-of-hot-water-in-bathtubs-and-whirlpool-bathtubs
- Content (UPC text at that URL): "The maximum hot water temperature discharging from the bathtub and whirlpool bathtub filler shall be limited to 120 degrees F (49 degrees C) by a device that complies with ASSE 1070/ASME A112.1070/CSA B125.70. The water heater thermostat shall not be considered a control for meeting this provision." In the 2021 UPC base Oregon adopted, a water heater conforming to ASSE 1084 is an additional permitted limiting means for 408.3, 409.4, and 410.3: https://www.oregon.gov/bcd/codes-stand/Documents/21opsc-change-summary.pdf
- Section number confirmed officially by the same BCD summary, which names "Sections 409.4 for bathtubs and whirlpool bathtubs and 410.3 for bidets" in the temperature-limitation discussion.

## Oregon amendment trail

- 2023 cycle: BCD's official "2023 OPSC, Summary of amendments" (effective Oct. 1, 2023) contains no amendment to 407.3, 408.3, 409.4, or 410.3 and no "scald" item at all. The only Section 408 change is 408.2, showerhead maximum flow reduced from 2.0 to 1.8 gpm at 80 psi per HB 2062 (2021), with the matching Table 401.3 row: https://www.oregon.gov/bcd/codes-stand/Documents/codechangesummary.pdf
- 2021 cycle: the anti-scald sections came in as model code without Oregon rewrite; the only motion was the model ASSE 1084 allowance described above: https://www.oregon.gov/bcd/codes-stand/Documents/21opsc-change-summary.pdf
- Statewide adoption boundary worth noting: UPC Appendix N, "Impact of Water Temperature on the Potential for Scalding and Legionella Growth," is not adopted statewide and is only available for local adoption, per OAR 918-750-0110(3)-(4): https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_918-750-0110 (mirror of the SOS OARD rule text).
- Related sections for the interiors chapter, both named in the BCD 2021 summary: 407.3 limits public lavatories (not private residential lavatories) to 120 degrees F; 410.3 requires a temperature-limiting device for bidets (the model UPC bidet limit is lower than the tub limit; verify the number in the adopted text before quoting it in the manuscript).
