## Inherited citations found wrong (each corrected in the text, filed in research/new/ch05.md)

1. Retaining-wall trigger cited as ORSC R404.1.1. The section is R404.4 (R404.1.1 is foundation walls).
2. "Where gravity discharge is not feasible, the drain discharges to a sump. Source: ORSC R405.2."
   R405.2 is wood foundations; R405.1 allows gravity or mechanical discharge and states no preference.
3. Gutters "required where roof drainage would discharge within 5 feet. Source: ORSC R903." The rule
   is R801.3 (expansive or collapsible soils: discharge 5 feet from foundation walls or to an
   approved system); R903.4 is roof drains at low points.
4. R506.2.3 stated as 10-mil Class A lapped 6 inches. Oregon's R506.2.3 ("Ground cover"): 6-mil
   polyethylene or approved Class I retarder, joints lapped not less than 12 inches.
5. R406.2 "where seasonal high groundwater is within 24 inches of the footing". The section's text has
   no number ("a high water table or other severe soil-water conditions are known to exist").
6. Expansive-soil definition sourced to IBC 1803.5.3. The ORSC carries it as R403.1.10.1 (Oregon
   number; R403.1.8.1 in the IRC), with "more than 10 percent" (not "10 percent or more").
7. ORS 455.058 cited for documentation on suspect soils. ORS 455.058 is the investigation fee for work
   begun without a permit.
8. "This sequence is practitioner guidance, not a single ORSC section" (backfill). R404.1.7 bars
   backfill until the wall has strength and is anchored or braced.
9. Fish-passage bed width "1.2 times the active channel width, plus 2 feet". OAR 635-412-0035(3)(a)(A)(i):
   "equal to or greater than the active channel width".
10. "The bearing surface must be clean ... Source: ORSC R403.1; ACI 318 26.5.2." R403.1 says footings
    bear on undisturbed natural soil or fill per R401.2; the frozen-soil bar is R403.1.4.1; the
    standing-water rule is ACI 318-19 26.5.2.1(b), read through Concrete International.
11. PP band table: "ORSC R401.4 requires geotechnical evaluation above 2,000 psf". R401.4.1: the table
    values (1,500 psf for clay) are assumed "in lieu of a complete geotechnical evaluation"; footnote
    b sends soils under 1,500 psf to a soils investigation. The 0.25 to 0.5 tsf row's "500 to 750
    psf" did not follow the stated 1,500-times rule (375 to 750). Consistency labels were one class
    stiff against Terzaghi and Peck (0.5 to 1.0 tsf is medium, not stiff).
