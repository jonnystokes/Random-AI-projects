## 6. Proposed WRITER_BRIEF changes (v5)

A. Under Do 2, after "Code entries live in the chapter that owns the subject": **"Routed items stay in the worklog. A cross-reference is one sentence naming one other chapter, by its title's topic, at most once per section, and only where the reader needs that chapter to do the step on this page. Do not pack two or three chapters into one sentence ("Slab joints belong to the foundation chapter, and the grinder to the tools chapter"), and do not dodge the count by naming a chapter as "the HVAC design", "the smart-home design" or "belongs to Resilience". B. Under "Name the source or state the fact": **"A study is named by its authors and year, or its title, in the sentence ("Yang and Moon, 2019"; "Effect of thermal-acoustic composite environments on comfort perceptions"); never by its journal or publisher ("studies in Building and Environment", "published by Springer"), and never as "one study", "another" or "every source"."**

C.
