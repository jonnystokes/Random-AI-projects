# The sample page: anchor bolts in four layers

One topic, four layers, no overlap. This page is the whole layer contract in one place. It
belongs near the front of the book, facing the page that explains the layers, and it is the
model a writer holds while writing any section.

---

## 10.6 Anchoring the wall to the foundation

**Simple**

The wall has to be tied to the foundation, or wind and an earthquake can slide it off the
footing. The tie is a row of steel bolts set into the wet concrete, sticking up through the
plate the wall sits on. You will see them as a line of nuts along the base of the wall before
the finishes cover them. An inspector can spot a missing or short bolt without a tool. After the
pour, the only fix is drilling in a replacement anchor.

**Applied**

1. Before the pour, mark the bolt positions on the forms: 6 feet apart or less, and one within
   12 inches of each end of every plate.
2. Set 1/2-inch anchor bolts in the wet concrete, 7 inches deep at minimum, plumb, with the
   threads clear of concrete.
3. After the pour, measure embedment and spacing on every bolt. Record the layout with a photo
   and a sketch in the binder.
4. When a bolt is missing or short, stop and call the engineer of record before the plate goes
   down. The repair is a drilled epoxy anchor sized by the engineer, not a field substitute.

**Professional**

The bolts transfer shear and uplift from the sill plate into the foundation. Embedment governs
pull-out, edge distance governs concrete breakout, and spacing sets how much plate each bolt
carries. A bolt close to each plate end holds the end down, because an unanchored end is where
the plate starts to lift when the wall racks. In Seismic Design Category D the plate washer
matters as much as the bolt: it keeps the plate from splitting under cross-grain bending when the
wall racks.

**Code**

```
Anchor bolts must tie the sill plate to the foundation.
  1/2-inch bolts minimum, embedded 7 inches, spaced 6 feet or less (4 feet for two-story in SDC D2), one bolt within 12 inches of each plate end, 3x3x0.229-inch plate washers.
  Source: ORSC R403.1.6 (2023 edition).
```

---

What makes it work: Simple tells you what the thing is and what it protects against, and names
something you can see. Applied is four steps you could do tomorrow, each with a value or a
check. Professional explains why those values are the values, without repeating them. Code
states them once, with the source. A reader can read one block and stop.
