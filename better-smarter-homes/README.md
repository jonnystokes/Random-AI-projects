# Better Smarter Homes: "Stone and Wire"

A technical owner-builder manual for building a long-life concrete home in Oregon. It is written for
five readers at once through four labeled layers: Simple, Applied, Professional and Code. This is a
manuscript, not software. `CLAUDE.md` tells AI agents where to start.

- `revision8/` is the active revision since 2026-10-26. `AI_TODO.md` is the recovery anchor, and
  `AGENT-STATE.md` covers the writer agents and how to carry on from another computer. Inside are
  `manuscript/` (36 chapters and 3 other files), `research/`, `worklogs/`, `tools/` and `exemplars/`.
- `revision7/` is the frozen baseline every Revision 8 rewrite starts from.
- `revision2/` to `revision6/` and `revision6_code_library/` are earlier full snapshots, all frozen.
- `writing-style-guide-v2.md` is the general prose guide every writer reads in full. Its home on the PC is `C:\Users\jon\Projects`, and files in revision8 give that path.
- `images/` holds the image-generation prompts and QA scripts. The 680 MB of generated PNG artwork is
  not in this repository. It stays on Jon's PC.
- `BUGS.md` lists bugs noticed and not yet fixed. The `external-input-*.md` files are outside reviews
  from 2026-10-27, to be acted on only when Jon says.

Only revision8 is edited. Status: in progress. Eleven of the 36 chapters are rewritten to the
Revision 8 standard, and the next steps are in `revision8/AGENT-STATE.md`.
