Notes on the two "+100% Authentic" listings at an identical $160.97 (ranks 3 and 4):
identical pricing and stock-photo titles are the signature of drop-shippers who
order from a retailer and have it shipped to you. Usually fine, sometimes cancelled
or shipped from a different source than stated.

## What to check on arrival from any eBay seller

Do this inside the 30-day Money Back Guarantee window, since there is no warranty:

1. Model label on the base reads Pro Plus, 112-0124-01, 120 V, 1200 W.
2. Sealing ring, float valve, steam release cap, condensation cup, trivet and inner
   pot are present; the inner pot is unscratched.
3. Water test: 2 cups water, Pressure Cook, Max, 5 minutes. It should reach
   pressure in under 10 minutes, hold without hissing, and count down.
4. Sous vide test: set 165°F, check with a thermometer after 30 minutes.
5. If anything fails, open an eBay "not as described" case immediately.
